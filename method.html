The framework

Method

We propose FedMEvo, a joint agentic memory evolution and communication optimization framework for wireless federated agent collaboration. FedMEvo explicitly incorporates wireless communication constraints into federated AME and jointly optimizes on-demand cross-agent memory exchange and wireless resource allocation according to task-aware memory utility. Moreover, a multi-granularity memory representation strategy is introduced into the co-optimization, enabling finer control over the semantic information and communication payload of exchanged memories.

The Main Contributions are summarized as follows:

  • We introduce FedMEvo, a communication-efficient federated AME framework for wireless federated agent collaboration. It jointly optimizes task-aware agentic-memory exchange and wireless resource allocation. (See Sec. III of the paper for the complete workflow; see Fig. 2 of the paper for the overview.)
  • We establish a rigorous mathematical characterization of the proposed co-optimization by deriving the unique closed-form optimal bandwidth allocation, revealing cross-agent communication coupling, and proving convergence of the discrete optimizer. (See Sec. IV of the paper for the complete mathematical analysis and proofs.)
  • Extensive experiments demonstrate the superior task performance and communication efficiency of FedMEvo, as well as its practical applicability in a real-world IoT scenario. (Table II in the paper presents the complete experimental roadmap.)
